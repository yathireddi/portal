Simple 4-Step Process

How ScholarConnect Works

Streamlined digital submission with transparent multi-stage review

1. Register Profile

Sign up with your academic score, category, institution, and income details.

2. Check Eligibility

Automated verification immediately tells you if you qualify and explains conditions.

3. Apply & Upload

Upload Aadhaar, marksheet, income proof, and bonafide securely in PDF/PNG format.

4. Review & Disbursal

Reviewers verify records, administrators sanction awards, and you track every milestone.

Why Choose ScholarConnect?

Engineered to bring transparency, speed, and equity to student financial support

Rule-Based Engine

Real-time matching algorithms compare your marks, category, and domicile with zero manual bias.

Secure Document Vault

Confidential documents are authenticated, cryptographically isolated, and safeguarded with strict RBAC.

Live Status Tracking

Instant SMS and portal notifications for every step: Under Review, Verification, or Sanction.

Three-Tier RBAC

Independent operational roles for Students, Scrutiny Reviewers, and System Administrators.
